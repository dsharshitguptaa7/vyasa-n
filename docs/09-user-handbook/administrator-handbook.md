# VYASA-N — ADMINISTRATOR & AUTHORITY USER HANDBOOK

**Target Audience:** Case Managers, Assistant Deans, Associate Deans, Deans, Committee Members  
**Platform Pillars:** Atharva Veda (NIVARAN-AI) & Institutional Admin Console  
**Document Type:** Case Management, Triage & Committee Workspace Operational Handbook  

---

## 1. Authority Login & Mandatory Two-Factor Onboarding

All university officials must authenticate with Two-Factor Authentication (TOTP).

### First-Login Onboarding Steps:
1. Navigate to `/login` (Institutional Authority Login).
2. Enter the temporary credentials provided by the university system administrator.
3. **Mandatory Password Change:** The system immediately prompts you to establish a permanent password. The password must contain at least 8 characters, an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, and a number. Temporary passwords cannot be reused.
4. **Authenticator Enrollment:**
   - The screen will display a secret QR code.
   - Open **Google Authenticator**, **Microsoft Authenticator**, or an equivalent app on your mobile phone.
   - Scan the QR code.
   - Enter the 6-digit verification code generated by your app.
   - **Save Your Recovery Codes:** The system presents 8 one-time emergency backup codes. Copy and store these in a secure offline location. If you lose your phone, these codes are required to regain access.

## 2. Case Manager Workflows

### 1. Intake Triage (`/modules/atharva-veda/nivaran/manager/queue`)
- Inspect new complaints arriving in `SUBMITTED` status.
- Review the machine learning model's suggested category and confidence percentage.
- If accurate, click **Accept Category**. If inaccurate, select the corrected category from the dropdown.
- Confirm Turn-Around-Time (TAT). Click **Dispatch Case**. The system routes the complaint to the designated Assistant Dean.

### 2. Final Case Closure Queue (`/modules/atharva-veda/nivaran/manager/closure-queue`)
- Inspect cases marked `RESOLVED` by an Assistant Dean, Associate Dean, or Committee.
- Verify that the resolution notes adequately address all student grievances and comply with university regulations.
- Enter formal **Closure Remarks**.
- Click **Finalize & Seal Case**. The system generates the official electronic dossier (e-File), hashes it with SHA-256, and seals the case permanently.

## 3. Assistant Dean Workflows (`/modules/atharva-veda/nivaran/assistant-dean/dashboard`)

### 1. Investigating Subject Cases
- Review active complaints filed under your academic subject cluster.
- Open the case detail view to inspect student affidavits, examination roll numbers, and chronological case history.

### 2. Taking Action:
- **Resolve Case:** If the matter is resolved (e.g. corrected marksheet issued), enter comprehensive resolution findings and click **Submit Resolution**.
- **Request Document:** If you need additional proof, click **Request Document**, specify the required records, and submit. The case pauses the SLA timer while waiting for the applicant.
- **Forward Upward:** If the case involves multi-departmental policy or conflicting statutes, click **Forward Case** to escalate to your Associate Dean.
- **Petition for Committee:** If an inquiry tribunal is required (e.g. disciplinary allegations), click **Request Committee Formation**. Detail the proposed terms of reference and petition the Associate Dean.

## 4. Associate Dean & Dean Committee Chartering

Associate Deans and Deans can directly charter inquiry panels or approve Assistant Dean petitions:
1. Navigate to `/modules/atharva-veda/nivaran/committees`.
2. Click **Charter New Committee**.
3. Enter:
   - **Committee Name** (e.g. *Fact-Finding Committee on Physics Examination Anomalies*)
   - **Terms of Reference**
   - **Select Chairperson** (choose from eligible university authorities)
   - **Select Members & Observers** (add faculty members to the active panel roster)
4. Click **Charter Committee**. The dedicated 8-tab workspace is initialized immediately.

---

## 5. Committee Workspace Operations (`/modules/atharva-veda/nivaran/committees/:id`)

Appointed committee members collaborate within a secure, confidential environment:
1. **Overview Tab:** Track chartered date, terms of reference, and linked grievance case.
2. **Members Tab:** View active panel roster, chair designation, and member responsibilities.
3. **Real-Time Deliberation Tab:** Conduct in-camera discussions and exchange real-time messages.
4. **Documents Tab:** Upload and inspect confidential evidence, student statements, and departmental reports.
5. **Polls & Voting Tab:**
   - Launch a formal resolution vote (Simple Majority, Two-Thirds, Unanimous).
   - Members cast secret ballots. Each vote records an immutable SHA-256 ballot hash.
6. **Meetings Tab:** Schedule virtual hearings. The system creates Google Meet conference links and calendar invites.
7. **Formal Decisions Tab:** The Chairperson records formal findings, consensus results, and official recommendations, automatically attaching the resolution to the parent grievance docket.
