## 1. Overview of the ANVESHAN Research Portal

The **ANVESHAN** portal ([`/modules/sama-veda`](file:///c:/Projects/VYASA/frontend/src/modules/sama-veda)) is CSJMU’s central research communication showcase. It gives university researchers high-visibility public dissemination for peer-reviewed papers, research bulletins, sponsored projects, and digital awards.

---

## 2. Exploring University Research Outputs

### 1. Research Publications Directory
- Navigate to `/modules/sama-veda/publications`.
- **Search & Filter:** Search by paper title, author name, journal title, or DOI.
- **Category Filter:** Filter by discipline: Basic Sciences, Applied Sciences, Engineering & Technology, Medical Sciences, Humanities & Social Sciences.
- **External Deep Links:** Click **View Article via DOI** to access the full-text publication on publisher websites (Elsevier, Springer, IEEE, Nature, ACS).

### 2. University Research Bulletins & In-Browser Viewer
- Navigate to `/modules/sama-veda/bulletins`.
- Browse bulletins organized chronologically by Volume and Issue number.
- Click any bulletin card to view the issue's editorial board, featured research highlights, and table of contents.
- Click **Read Bulletin** to launch the in-browser PDF viewer. Use zoom controls, page jumping, or click **Download PDF** to save an offline copy.

### 3. Faculty Researcher Profiles
- Navigate to `/modules/sama-veda/researchers`.
- Locate your faculty profile. Profiles display:
  - Full Name & Departmental Affiliation
  - Verified Researcher Badge (confirmed by Research Office)
  - Scopus Author ID and ORCID Profile link
  - Cumulative indexed publications list

## 3. Public Verification of Digital Felicitation Certificates

University scholars and faculty members awarded research excellence citations or travel grants receive an official digital certificate embedded with a unique QR code.

### How to Verify a Certificate:
1. Scan the QR code printed on the physical certificate, or navigate to `/modules/sama-veda/felicitation`.
2. Enter the official Certificate Number (e.g., `CSJMU/SAMA/2026/0142`).
3. Click **Verify Authenticity**.
4. The system inspects the central database and displays:
   - Recipient Full Name & Email
   - Achievement Title & Award Category
   - Issue Date & Registrar's Digital Signature
   - Direct download button for the authentic official PDF certificate.

> [!WARNING]
> **Revocation Alert:** If a certificate has been cancelled or revoked, the verification screen will display an orange warning alert indicating the revocation date and administrative rationale. PDF downloading is automatically disabled.
